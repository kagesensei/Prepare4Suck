# Prepare4Suck

**[Play it in your browser](https://kagesensei.github.io/Prepare4Suck/)**

A funny little game I created at the suggestion of a friend who's recovering from knee replacement surgery. He's continuing to get stronger while people keep trying to keep him on the couch. So he wanted a game of himself at the gym fighting off couch potatoes trying to hit him with pillows and cake.

## How to play
Use the link above, or download `prepare-for-suck.html` and open it in any modern browser. No install, no build step. It works with a keyboard and mouse, or on a phone or tablet (turn it sideways).

Survive from week 5 to week 52 of recovery. Keep your heart rate up, knock the cake back, and stay out of the recliner. Between weeks, spend your reps on upgrades. Progress saves automatically after every week you survive, so you can close the tab and pick up where you left off.

### Heart rate is everything
Your heart rate drops whenever you slack off. Below 95 bpm the brain fog sets in: everything blurs, you move like you're wading through pudding, and your fatigue climbs. Above 140 bpm your fatigue actually goes down. Resting makes it worse. Pushing harder is how you recover.

Fatigue is your health bar. If it hits 100%, you're sent to the recliner.

Every so often Brock, your trainer, blows his whistle and calls out an exercise. Do it fast and you get pumped: bonus damage and bonus reps.

When the Pain meter fills, unleash **The answer is always more pain** for a few seconds of unstoppable berserk mode.

Before each boss week you get a **chess break**: find checkmate in one move for bonus reps to spend on upgrades. Or skip it.

## The Comfort Brigade
They love you. They want you resting for a full year. Fight them off.

| Enemy | What she does |
| --- | --- |
| Pillow Nana | Throws pillows that knock you out for a nap |
| Cake Granny | Throws cake. You eat it. Fatigue goes up |
| Afghan Knitter | Throws blankets that tangle you up and slow you down |
| The Chorus | Travels in threes, chanting "You're overdoing it!" and pulling you toward the recliner |

Swing your kettlebell at anything they throw to knock it right back at them. And don't let any of them get close enough for a hug.

### Bosses
Some weeks bring someone special. We won't spoil what they do.

* Week 12: **The Group Moderator**
* Week 24: **Ice** and **Elevate**
* Week 52: **The Grand Matriarch of the Recliner**

### Keyboard and mouse
| Action | Control |
| --- | --- |
| Move | WASD or arrow keys |
| Aim | Mouse |
| Kettlebell swing (also knocks thrown items back) | Left click or J |
| Battle ropes | Right click or K |
| Squat slam | Space |
| The answer is always more pain (when the Pain meter is full) | Q |
| Pause | P or Esc |
| Sound on/off | M |

### Touch
| Action | Control |
| --- | --- |
| Move | Drag anywhere on the left side of the screen |
| Aim | Automatic, at the closest nana |
| Swing, Ropes, Slam | Hold the buttons on the right |
| Pain | Tap when its ring is full |
| Pause | Button in the top right corner |
| Wake up from a nap | Tap repeatedly |
