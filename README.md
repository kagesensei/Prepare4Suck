# Prepare4Suck
A funny little game I created at the suggestion of a friend who's recovering from knee replacement surgery. He's continuing to get stronger while people keep trying to keep him on the couch. So he wanted a game of himself at the gym fighting off couch potatoes trying to hit him with pillows and cake.

## How to play
Open `prepare-for-suck.html` in any modern browser. No install, no build step. It works with a keyboard and mouse, or on a phone or tablet (turn it sideways).

Survive from week 5 to week 52 of recovery. Keep your heart rate up, knock the cake back, and stay out of the recliner. Between weeks, spend your reps on upgrades. Progress saves automatically after every week you survive, so you can close the tab and pick up where you left off.

### Keyboard and mouse
| Action | Control |
| --- | --- |
| Move | WASD or arrow keys |
| Aim | Mouse |
| Kettlebell swing (also knocks thrown items back) | Left click or J |
| Battle ropes | Right click or K |
| Squat slam | Space |
| The answer is always more pain (when the Pain meter is full) | Q |
| Pause | P or Esc |
| Sound on/off | M |

### Touch
| Action | Control |
| --- | --- |
| Move | Drag anywhere on the left side of the screen |
| Aim | Automatic, at the closest nana |
| Swing, Ropes, Slam | Hold the buttons on the right |
| Pain | Tap when its ring is full |
| Pause | Button in the top right corner |
| Wake up from a nap | Tap repeatedly |
